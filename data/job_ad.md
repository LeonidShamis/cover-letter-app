# Job: Senior AI Manager (Development Leader)

Company: Pathfindr.ai

We don't just advise'we design, build, and deliver. Now we're looking for a confident, client-facing leader to help scale that impact.

**What You'll Be Doing:**

This is a delivery leadership role with deep technical roots. You'll be the engine behind our engineering teams'guiding architecture, managing people, and ensuring the work gets delivered at quality and pace.

- Lead and grow a high-performing development team (onshore and offshore), ensuring engineering standards and delivery discipline
- Take ownership of solution delivery across AI, automation, and internal tooling projects
- Architect and evolve our cloud-based infrastructure (mostly Azure), including DevOps pipelines and internal frameworks
- Drive consistent execution, from sprint planning and team workflows through to post-deployment QA and scalability
- Work cross-functionally with AI consultants, delivery leads, and client stakeholders to shape technical direction and unblock teams
- Support early-stage prototyping and POCs to help validate AI concepts in-market
- Continuously improve delivery playbooks, frameworks, and team processes as we scale

**What You Bring:**

- Strong technical background in software engineering, system architecture, or DevOps
- Experience building and leading teams that ship production-grade platforms or products
- Comfort working across AI tools and LLMs (we don't expect you to be a data scientist, but experience delivering AI features or building around them is a plus)
- Hands-on with cloud infra (Azure preferred), CI/CD, and internal tool development
- You know how to translate complexity into structure and keep projects moving
- Confident managing multiple workstreams and delivery paths
- Comfortable in fast-moving, ambiguous startup environments where you'll wear many hats

**Why Join Pathfindr?**

This is not a role for someone who's managed projects around tech. We need a technical delivery leader who has built the platforms, run the infrastructure, and led from within the tech itself ' not just tracked it.
At Pathfindr, we:

- Move fast and work with cutting-edge AI technology.
- Give you ownership over high-impact AI and automation projects.
- Ensure you work with top-tier clients across government, enterprise, and startups.

If you're looking for a role where you can lead delivery, scale execution, and build world-class AI-driven solutions, we'd love to hear from you.

**Employer questions**:

Your application will include the following questions:

- Beyond what's outlined in your CV, can you share your personal interest in AI? 
- How have you been exploring or experimenting with AI, and what excites you most about its potential?
